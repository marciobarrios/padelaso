# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Groups of friends who play padel together and primarily use the app on mobile devices before, during, and after matches.

## Product Purpose

Padelaso makes it easy for friend groups to record matches, celebrate memorable moments, and follow playful stats and rankings over time.

## Brand Personality

Social, playful, and retro. The experience should feel lighthearted and communal while keeping match data clear and trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Avoid corporate dashboards, overly serious competitive-sports products, and interfaces that turn friendly group play into an intimidating professional ranking system.

## Design Principles

- Keep the primary mobile task obvious and quick to complete.
- Make stats trustworthy without losing the playful group atmosphere.
- Use emphasis sparingly so rankings remain easy to scan.
- Prefer familiar interaction patterns over decorative complexity.
- Celebrate participation and shared moments, not only winners.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Take WCAG AA principles into account where practical, including keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, and status cues that do not rely only on color. Prioritize clear, touch-friendly mobile interactions.
